# IoT-IDS project hub

IoT-IDS contains two deliberately separate systems: a 22-feature, 10-class live
edge IDS/IPS and a 12-feature binary SFAF cross-dataset research study. Their
models, scalers, metrics, and claims are never interchangeable.

## Durable boundaries

- Dataset and real-traffic evidence is external to Git and may be unavailable.
- Synthetic live-model accuracy is not real-traffic validation.
- SFAF headline values remain withdrawn until the corrected rerun.
- Raspberry Pi claims require measurements on identified Pi hardware.
- Deployable artifacts are ONNX or C; do not ship pickle artifacts.
- External/destructive actions retain action-specific confirmation gates.
